HOW TO PLAY

Aim — drag left or right across the top half of the screen.

Throw — put a finger on the lower half and pull it down. The further you pull, the harder the throw. The ring shows roughly how far it will go and where it would land on open ground. Let go to throw.

Discs — D, M and P: a driver flies furthest and finishes left; a midrange flies straighter; a putter is for the basket. Inside 20 m a putter throws a putt.

Hyzer / anhyzer — tilt the disc. Hyzer bends the flight left, anhyzer bends it right.

Gold line — runs along the ground from you to the basket, and a beam of light marks the basket. You can turn it off in the menu.

Players — on the player screen, tap HUMAN or CPU beside each name. Humans pass the phone; computer players line up and throw by themselves.

Trees knock discs down. The lake costs a penalty throw and you play on from where the disc went in. Furthest from the basket throws next. Tap during a flight to speed it up.
